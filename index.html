SplitMate Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SplitMate (the "App"), provided by Dash's Solutions ("we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses and protects your information when you use the App. By using SplitMate you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us:

  • Account details such as your name and email address, if you create an account.
  • Expense data you enter, such as group names, expense descriptions, amounts, dates and the people you split costs with.
  • Messages you send us when you contact support.

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic device information such as device model, operating system version and app version.
  • Crash reports and performance data that help us fix bugs.

SplitMate does not collect your precise location. It does not access your contacts, photos, microphone or camera unless you choose to use a feature that needs that permission, and we ask for your permission first.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide the App and calculate who owes what in your groups.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent misuse.
  • To fix bugs and improve the App.
  • To respond to your questions and support requests.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Other group members: expense details you add to a shared group are visible to the other members of that group.
  • Service providers: trusted companies that host our servers, store data or provide crash reporting. They may only use your data to provide services to us.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

SplitMate does not process payments between users. Any money you send to friends happens outside the App.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time by emailing us at the address below. We will act on verified requests within 30 days. Some information may be kept longer if the law requires it.

5. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under South Africa's POPIA or the EU GDPR) you may have the right to access your data, correct it, delete it, object to certain processing or withdraw consent. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

SplitMate is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International data transfers

Our service providers may store or process data in countries other than your own. Where this happens we take steps to protect your data in line with this policy.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
